## Consensus issues (raised by 2+ referees)
1. **Direct IT vs IV comparison of immune/translational data** — R2-1; R4-1. Outcome: new Extended Data Fig 7.
2. **Transcript-only evidence for mechanistic claims needs protein corroboration** — R2-3 (HLA/pre-existing immunity); R4-11 (Fc receptors). Outcome: claim softened (R2-3); rebutted for lack of tissue (R4-11).
3. **Practicality/safety of the IT procedure** — R2-14; R4-3, R4-4 (R4 also in general comments). Outcome: Methods details and grade 1–2 AE data added.
4. **Interpretation of Treg/M2-associated benefit** — R1-2 (biomarker feasibility); R2-7 (was it unexpected?). Outcome: Discussion addition; "unexpected" framing kept.
5. **Nivolumab PK in IV-ipilimumab arm** — R1-3 (observation); R2-10, R2-11. Outcome: rebutted; extra PD-1 data in letter only.
6. **Abstract "hypotheses" typo** — R1-7; R2-16.
