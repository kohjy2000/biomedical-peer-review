# Author-facing review template

Use the user or journal's supplied template when present. Otherwise use the connected-prose structure below. Keep detailed literature discussion and supporting analyses in the working dossier and checked full-review.md. Preserve the complete draft and checked detailed review before shortening the submission copy.

For the final report, aim for 600–1,000 words across Overall Assessment, Major Comments, Minor Comments and Recommendation. This is a working target, not a hard limit or a comment quota. Exceed it when independent consequential issues cannot be explained adequately within it; record the reason in editing-notes.md. The detailed draft/full review has no word target. User or journal requirements override this default.

## Overall Assessment

[One compact paragraph: supported contribution, why it matters, central uncertainty and revision priority.]

## Major Comments

### 1. [Short title identifying the scientific issue]

[One focused paragraph connecting the manuscript-specific problem, its scientific consequence, and the minimum sufficient correction or evidence. Include an alternative only when it resolves the same issue and leaves an appropriate contribution. State optional work as optional in prose. Do not display Problem / Why it matters / Action or Plan A / Plan B fields. Use a short list only when needed to distinguish inseparable required components.]

[Repeat for independent consequential issues, ordered by importance and dependencies. Do not delete or combine unrelated critical issues just to meet the length target.]
