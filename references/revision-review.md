# Revision Review Workflow and Template

Revision review is not a new initial review. Judge whether the authors resolved the previously identified claim-critical issues and whether the revision introduced new contradictions.

Read the existing review state first when available. Use [review-state-template.md](review-state-template.md) to preserve resolution status and [review-framework.md](review-framework.md) to reassess only new or materially changed claim branches.

## Required inputs

Use, when available:

- prior reviewer report;
- point-by-point response;
- revised manuscript with clean or tracked version;
- updated figures and supplements;
- editor instructions for the revision round.

## Resolution ledger

For each prior Major Comment, record:

```markdown
## Prior issue [number/title]
- Issue ID:
- Original concern:
- Affected H/M/E identifiers:
- Author response:
- Manuscript change and location:
- Resolution: Fully resolved / Partially resolved / Unresolved / Not assessable
- Evidence for judgment:
- Remaining validity or venue-completeness gap:
- Remaining action, if any:
```

Do not accept the response letter as proof that the manuscript changed. Verify the revised text, analysis, figure, or data directly.

## Decision rules

- Drop fully resolved issues from the author-facing report unless a one-line acknowledgment helps explain the recommendation.
- Re-state partially resolved issues in their current form; do not paste the original comment unchanged.
- Distinguish validity-critical evidence from venue-critical completeness, and determine whether a narrowed claim would remain appropriate for the current journal.
- Check whether new data created denominator, sample-count, label, legend, or methods/results inconsistencies.
- Avoid expanding the review to unrelated new demands unless the revision created a consequential new problem.
- Do not rebuild unaffected claim branches or repeat a broad literature search without a material reason.
- Update the state ledger after resolving each prior issue and before drafting the next-round report.
- If the remaining issues are wording, internal consistency, or statistical clarification, classify them accordingly rather than preserving the prior severity by inertia.

## Revision review template

After drafting either revision-review template, apply S5 in [workflow.md](workflow.md) before delivery. Use the resolution ledger as the S3-equivalent input and read [final-pruning.md](final-pruning.md) only for that final pass.

```markdown
Overall Assessment

[State how substantially the revision improved the manuscript, which prior central concerns were resolved, and the severity of what remains.]

Major Comments

[Include only unresolved claim-critical issues. Omit this section when none remain.]

1. [Remaining issue]
[What was improved.] [What remains unsupported or unclear.] [Exact action needed.]

Minor Comments

1. [Internal inconsistency, terminology, legend, number, or residual claim calibration.]

Recommendation

[Decision and concise logic. State whether remaining evidence is validity-critical, venue-critical, or resolvable by textual or analytical revision.]
```

## Short acceptability-check template

```markdown
Overall Assessment

The revised manuscript has addressed the major points raised in the previous round and is substantially improved in [evidence/scope/transparency]. The remaining issues are limited to [brief description] and should be resolvable without major new experimentation.

Minor Comments

1. [Specific correction.]
2. [Specific correction.]

Recommendation

Minor Revision. [One-sentence rationale.]
```

## Confidential editor comment for revisions

State:

1. whether the central prior concerns were actually resolved;
2. whether remaining problems are conceptual, experimental, statistical, or textual;
3. whether another full review round is necessary;
4. whether claim reduction would leave a sufficient advance for the journal.
