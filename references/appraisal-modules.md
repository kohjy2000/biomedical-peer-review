# Conditional Appraisal Modules

Read only the modules relevant to the study types and claims identified in S0. These sources supplement, but do not replace, the three S2b judgments.

## Use boundary

- **Reporting guidelines** identify information needed for transparent interpretation. A checked item does not prove that the method was sound; a missing item may make the result Not assessable without proving that the method was poor.
- **Critical-appraisal or risk-of-bias tools** inform S2b Pass A for the affected result. They do not replace Pass B claim judgment or Pass C claim-network judgment.
- Apply a module only to the result and inference for which it was designed. Do not calculate a generic quality score or turn every unchecked item into a review issue.
- When a module exposes a consequential omission, return to the affected `E→M`, M, or bridge, revise the appraisal, and record the resulting issue or the reason for setting it aside.
- Verify the current official version before treating any item as a standard.

## Router

| Study or result type | Reporting source | Appraisal source when needed | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General life-science or preclinical experiment | NIH Principles for Preclinical Research; MDAR | Claim-specific design and analysis appraisal | Materials, experimental unit, replicates, exclusions, randomization/blinding, statistics, data and code |
| In vivo animal experiment | ARRIVE 2.0 | SYRCLE risk-of-bias tool when bias appraisal is material | Animal-specific design, allocation, blinding, unit, procedures, outcomes, exclusions |
| Randomized clinical trial | CONSORT 2025 | Cochrane RoB 2 | Randomization, deviations, missing outcomes, outcome measurement, selective reporting |
| Observational intervention-effect study | STROBE | ROBINS-I | Confounding, selection, intervention classification, deviations, missing data, outcome measurement, selective reporting |
| Diagnostic-accuracy study | STARD 2015 | QUADAS-3 when formal bias and applicability appraisal is needed | Participants, index test, target condition, analysis, and estimate-level applicability |
| Systematic review or meta-analysis | PRISMA 2020 | A review-method appraisal tool appropriate to the question | Search, selection, risk of bias, synthesis, certainty, reporting |
| Clinical prediction model or healthcare AI | TRIPOD+AI | PROBAST+AI | Participants/data, predictors, outcome, analysis, evaluation, applicability and fairness |
| Medical-imaging AI | CLAIM 2024 in addition to TRIPOD+AI when applicable | PROBAST+AI | Imaging-specific data handling, reference standards, evaluation and reproducibility |

Use the official source rather than reproducing its full checklist in the dossier. Record the module, why it applies, the omission or bias signal found, and the S2b judgment that was reopened.
